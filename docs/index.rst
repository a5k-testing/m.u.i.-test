Welcome to the documentation for **microG unofficial installer** |release|.

`microG unofficial installer <https://github.com/micro5k/microg-unofficial-installer>`_ is a
flashable zip that installs `microG <https://microg.org/>`_ on Android devices running versions
2.2 through 16 — no root wizardry required, just a compatible recovery and about ten minutes of
your time. *(The ten minutes is for reading the instructions. The installation itself is faster.
Unless something goes wrong. But let's stay positive.)*

**What it does:** It replaces the proprietary Google Play Services with microG, a free and
open-source re-implementation that keeps your apps happy without handing your entire digital
life to a single corporation. Think of it as a polite but firm eviction notice for Google.

**The ideal:** Privacy, freedom, and the belief that your phone should work for *you* — not
the other way around. This project is built on the principle that open-source software can be
powerful, accessible, and — as this very documentation hopes to prove — occasionally funny.
